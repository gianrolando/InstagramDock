InstagramDock 0.7 — macOS 13 or later

1. Drag InstagramDock.app to Applications.
2. Open the app and sign in to Instagram.
3. If macOS blocks this download, check System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   Open Anyway after the first attempt, if available and you trust the source.
   This app is ad hoc signed and not notarized. Do not disable Gatekeeper.
Window size and position are remembered after resizing or moving the window.

4. Closing the window keeps the app running. Click the Dock icon to reopen it.
5. Command+Q quits the app and stops badge updates.
6. Command+Shift+D shows or hides the developer panel.
   You can also choose View > Show Developer Panel.

The badge is inferred from the loaded page. + means a lower bound.
After reading the last message, allow roughly 10 seconds for the badge to clear.
No macOS notification banners are included. Instagram may change its interface.
Universal Apple Silicon/Intel package. Intel compiled, not tested on hardware.
App-owned UI is English; Instagram and system dialogs use their own settings.
Not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. MIT license included.

Home layout: the app limits the feed tab strip when it overlaps the right-hand
profile column. This workaround recognizes English and Italian page labels.

Credits: choose InstagramDock > About InstagramDock. The window includes the
app icon, version, copyright, and GitHub/License buttons. License opens offline.
